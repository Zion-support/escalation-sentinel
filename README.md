## What it does

Escalation Sentinel watches ticket threads, Slack channels, and account activity for escalation signals — repeated contact, legal mentions, executive complaints — and briefs the right owner before it blows up.

## Key features

- **Escalation signals** — repetition, sentiment slope, legal/exec keywords.
- **Owner briefing** — full context summary delivered to the right person.
- **Response SLA watch** — aging high-risk threads surfaced hourly.
- **De-escalation templates** — proven responses attached to each alert.
- **Post-mortem log** — every escalation tracked to resolution and learning.

## Who it's for

Support leaders and account managers who get surprised by escalations.
